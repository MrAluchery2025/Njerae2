My Music

Welcome to my musical world, where soulful melodies meet raw emotions and every song tells a story. Explore my music, feel the rhythm, and discover the sounds that speak to the heart and soul. Every melody holds a feeling, every lyric carries a message, and every song invites you to be part of the journey.

Aki Sioni

Aki Sioni is a soulful song by Njerae that expresses deep emotions through heartfelt lyrics and soothing melodies, capturing the beauty and challenges of love.

OTD

“I think about you on the daily” captures the song’s theme of longing and constantly thinking about someone you love. OTD by Njerae blends soulful melodies with heartfelt emotions, expressing the desire to reconnect with someone special.

colors

Colors by Njerae featuring Bensoul is a vibrant love song that expresses attraction and the desire to be close to someone special. The line “Come my way, I’m here waiting” captures the longing for love and connection through soulful melodies and catchy rhythms.

On My Own

On My Own by Njerae is a soulful song that explores the journey of self-discovery and independence. The lyrics convey a sense of empowerment and resilience, as the artist navigates life’s challenges and embraces personal growth.

Ingia Ndani

Ingia Ndani by Njerae is a heartfelt song that delves into the complexities of love and relationships. The lyrics express vulnerability and the desire for emotional connection, resonating with listeners who have experienced the highs and lows of romantic entanglements.

Let them Know

Let Them Know by Njerae is a powerful anthem that encourages self-expression and standing up for oneself. The song’s lyrics convey a message of confidence and assertiveness, inspiring listeners to embrace their true selves and make their voices heard.
